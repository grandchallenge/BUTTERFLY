# BUTTERFLY Programme Charter 0.1.0

## Identity and purpose

- Programme: BUTTERFLY CLOSURE
- Identifier: `BFC`
- Repository: `grandchallenge/BUTTERFLY`
- State: `adopted` (research work packages and claims remain individually gated)
- Purpose: discover, falsify, certify within declared numerical scope, and
  compile complementary low-rank computation while preserving deterministic
  reference and rejection paths.

## Bounded scope

The technical scope and deliverables BFC-D1 through BFC-D10 are defined in
`PROGRAMME_SPEC.md`. G0 through G5 are falsification gates, not established
results. The initial admission subject is the deterministic reference stack and
its reproducible, narrowly bounded baseline observations.

| Phase | BUTTERFLY artifact or condition |

| Charter | This charter, ADR-0001, explicit scope and acceptance criteria |

## Work graph

The BFC-001 through BFC-100 dependency spine in `WORK_BREAKDOWN.md` is the
programme work graph. Each node receives its own exact-subject records. No
downstream node inherits promotion from an upstream node.

## Initial acceptance criteria

Admission requires a clean, reproducible reference baseline; closed and
semantically validated governance records; protected GitHub controls; distinct
Adversary and Referee logical audit passes, which may be staffed by the same
system; Human Steward authorization; cross-repository
registration; and protected/public readback. Research deliverables may remain
gated if their unresolved obligations are explicit.

## Claim boundary

Local numerical certificates report bounded software measurements. They do not
prove a general theorem or constitute MATHCERT certification. No scientific,
mathematical, deployment, hardware, novelty, priority, safety, manufacturing,
product, or commercial authority follows from programme admission.
