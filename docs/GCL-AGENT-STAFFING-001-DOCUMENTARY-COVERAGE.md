# GCL Agent Staffing Documentary Coverage

BUTTERFLY prospectively follows `GCL-AGENT-STAFFING-001` v1.0.0 under
`GI-STEWARD-0003`. The exact adoption binding is recorded in
`governance/GCL-AGENT-STAFFING-001-ADOPTION.json`.

For non-reserved work, one system may staff multiple roles through distinct
role-scoped logical passes. Each pass records the role, criteria, exact subject,
mode, finding, evidence, unresolved obligations, and unique `logical_pass_id`.
An authoring system's later Adversary or Referee pass is
`non_authoring_read_only`. Material changes invalidate affected findings.

Historical qualification and admission artifacts are preserved byte-for-byte
because they identify the evidence, policy, and outstanding conditions recorded
at their creation. In particular, the phrase “distinct-session Adversary and
Referee findings” in `artifacts/BFC-REQUALIFICATION-002.json` is a historical
obligation statement, not a current staffing instruction. If that candidate is
requalified, the operative obligation is distinct Adversary and Referee logical
passes under the canonical schema; it does not require a different system,
invocation, task, model, account, or human.

Likewise, generic “independent review” wording in earlier programme, evidence,
claim, negative-knowledge, and admission records means an applicable governed
review outcome. It does not override the canonical staffing standard. Explicit
organizational-independence exceptions, Human Steward reserved authority, and
MATHCERT certification boundaries remain binding.
