# Release Policy

BUTTERFLY is an incubation candidate. No release, package publication, Pages
deployment, or scientific claim promotion is authorized by the onboarding
candidate or by green continuous-integration results.

A release is eligible only after the relevant artifacts are admitted through
the protected programme lifecycle and the Human Steward has authorized the
exact release candidate. Release tags are immutable.

Every eligible release must contain or link to:

- checksums for every released artifact;
- deterministic replay instructions and the exact source identity;
- the admitted evidence bundle and bounded claim ledger;
- a dependency inventory or software bill of materials; and
- build provenance and attestations produced by an authorized release path.

Numerical certificates are software evidence only. They do not constitute
MATHCERT certification, proof, novelty, priority, safety, deployment, hardware,
or commercial authority.
