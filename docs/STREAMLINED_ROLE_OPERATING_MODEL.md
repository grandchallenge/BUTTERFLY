# Streamlined role operating model

This is the normative staffing and approval contract for BUTTERFLY. It applies
to every onboarded human or automated agent unless a narrower binding rule is
identified for a specific subject.

## Core rule: separate functions, not identities

The council names are audit lenses. They do not create eight staffing seats.
The same Codex system is licensed to wear multiple non-reserved roles, including
all applicable roles in one task.

No identity multiplication is required. Role separation does not require a
different person, model, process, conversation, GitHub account, pull-request
review, or approval click. It requires an inspectable change of audit mode:

- declare the role and logical pass identifier;
- bind the pass to an exact subject;
- apply the role's stated criteria;
- keep review passes read-only;
- record findings, counterevidence, and unresolved obligations; and
- rerun affected passes after a material subject change.

The logical pass identifier distinguishes audit phases. It does not assert that
different infrastructure, credentials, or conversations were used.

## Role lenses

| Role | Required lens |
|---|---|
| Axiomatist | Definitions, preconditions, and internal consistency |
| Cartographer | Dependencies, experiment graph, and affected surfaces |
| Compiler | Implementation, runtime path, and contract preservation |
| Verifier | Tests, tolerances, reproducibility, and certificates |
| Adversary | Counterexamples, hostile cases, and failure behavior |
| Formalist | Proof obligations, assumptions, and claim boundary |
| Amanuensis | Decisions, terminology, provenance, and document consistency |
| Referee | Whether the accumulated evidence satisfies the stated gate |

A role may be marked not applicable with a reason. A blanket set of office
approvals is not a prerequisite for routine work.

## Work classification

### Routine bounded

A reversible change within existing contracts and authority, with no new
promoted claim or reserved decision. The system may implement, test, perform
applicable role passes, merge through protected checks, and verify protected
readback under standing or exact delegation. Human involvement is zero by
default.

### Substantive

A change to a public contract, evidence gate, authority boundary, or material
research result. The system performs the applicable role passes and records the
decision in an ADR when required. Human involvement is still zero unless the
decision is explicitly reserved.

### Reserved

A governing contract expressly assigns the judgment to the Human Steward or
another human authority. Examples include granting new external authority or
making a human attestation. Automation prepares one consolidated exact-subject
decision and, once supplied, may faithfully record and execute it. Automation
must not invent the decision or treat silence as assent.

## Authorship and review

`non-author` describes the audit pass, not the permanent identity of the
system. After authoring, the system may switch to a declared non-authoring pass.
During that pass it reads and evaluates the exact candidate but does not edit
it. If correction is needed, the pass ends, the candidate is changed, and the
affected audit is rerun.

Adversary and Referee findings use distinct logical pass identifiers because
they answer different questions. They may name the same underlying system as
reviewer. A Human Steward record, when actually required, must identify the
human authority and may be transcribed or mechanically executed by the system.

## Completion rule

Completion means applicable evidence passes, protected integration succeeds,
readback agrees with the intended exact state, and unresolved obligations are
truthfully reported. It does not mean that green CI, a numerical certificate,
or automated consensus has created human authority or mathematical
certification.
