# Contributing to BUTTERFLY

All changes use pull requests against protected `main`. Identify the affected
BFC deliverable, work package, authoritative artifacts, claim boundary,
evidence route, promotion gate, reversal condition, and next finite obligation.

Classify a change as `routine_bounded`, `substantive`, or `reserved`. For
routine bounded work, the Codex system may author, test, perform multiple
role-scoped audit passes, merge through protected checks, and verify readback
under standing or exact delegation. No fresh human click, arbitrary approval
count, separate agent, or separate account is required.

Substantive work receives the applicable role passes in proportion to its claim
and risk boundaries. The same system may wear multiple non-reserved roles; the
separation lies in declared criteria, logical pass identifiers, exact-subject
evidence, and a read-only review mode. Human action is reserved for decisions
that the governing contract explicitly assigns to a human authority.
Automation may faithfully record and execute such a decision, but may not
invent human authorization, mathematical certification, scientific claim
promotion, or authority outside this programme.

See `docs/STREAMLINED_ROLE_OPERATING_MODEL.md` for the normative contract.
