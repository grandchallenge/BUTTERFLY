# WP2 — Hierarchy and Topology Discovery

Implement candidate input/output orderings and trees.

Minimum methods:

- balanced index trees;
- geometry-aware recursive partitioning;
- spectral ordering from an interaction graph;
- balanced k-means hierarchy;
- permutation search with a hardware-cost penalty.

The optimization target must combine approximation tail, maximum rank, balance, and runtime cost. Add topology stability metrics across random seeds and nearby operator states.

Do not introduce differentiable sorting before the nonlearned baselines and controls are complete.
