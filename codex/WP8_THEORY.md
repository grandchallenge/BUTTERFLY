# WP8 — Theory Programme

Formalize and prove, where possible:

1. global approximation bounds from complementary block errors;
2. perturbation bounds for products of sparse factors;
3. sufficient conditions for conditional-topology transfer;
4. statistical guarantees for sampled closure estimates;
5. downstream utility bounds through Lipschitz continuations;
6. no-regret selection among dense, butterfly, and corrected execution.

Every theorem must state access assumptions, tree assumptions, rank definition, norm, probability, constants, and failure modes. Pair each theorem with a numerical falsification test.
