# BUTTERFLY programme governance

## Authority

BUTTERFLY is a candidate standalone GCL research programme. Its proposed
authority is limited to its own work-package state and bounded,
Referee-governed research-result promotion.

It has no authority to certify mathematics, establish novelty or priority,
authorize production or safety-critical deployment, validate hardware, or
make manufacturing, product, or commercial claims. MATHCERT alone may admit a
mathematical certificate through an independently governed route.

The authority order is the INTELLECT Constitution and effective schedules,
admitted cross-programme standards, programme adoption records, repository
profiles, and then GitHub projections. Lower layers cannot enlarge authority.

## Current state

`BFC-ADMISSION-001` is `candidate`. All scientific work packages and claims
remain unpromoted. A green check, mergeable pull request, local certificate,
benchmark, issue, release, or package is evidence only.

## Admission

Admission requires an exact-subject packet, distinct non-author Adversary and
Referee findings from separate sessions, authenticated Human Steward
authorization, protected integration, settings readback, and a final public
readback. Any subject or packet identity change invalidates prior findings and
authorization.

Automation may assemble, validate, route, and replay evidence. It may not
approve, merge, certify, promote, admit, activate, or authorize publication.
